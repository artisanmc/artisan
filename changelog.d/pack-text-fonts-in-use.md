- **Changed**: The resource pack no longer carries a text font for every size at every height
  (about 1,070 fonts, most of a pack's weight). It keeps size 8 at every height and writes the
  sizes 12 to 32 only at the heights the menus' texts and labels use: a pack of 9.4 MB drops
  to about 2.5 MB. Moving a text of another size than 8, or changing its size, now changes the
  pack, like moving an image button.
