- **Added**: Symbols in menu title texts and labels: arrows, maths, box drawing, shapes, dingbats and
  the other symbols the chat draws through Unifont (`↪`, `⌨`, `▤`…) now show in titles instead of
  a missing-glyph box. The resource pack carries only the symbols the Projects' texts use (about
  300 KB once one is used, nothing otherwise), drawn from GNU Unifont like the chat; titles centre
  and wrap them with their real widths, and the editor previews them. A symbol that only arrives
  through a placeholder at runtime is not in the pack.
