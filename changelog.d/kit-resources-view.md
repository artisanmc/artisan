- **Added**: The Resources page has a **UI kit** view: every piece of the kit's base with its
  size and who drew it (Artisan or this server), the server's other kit images and its themes.
  In the `ui-kit` module a piece is redrawn in the pixel editor from Artisan's image, or given
  back to Artisan; from another module the view opens the kit module, or creates it.
- **Added**: A composed image's composer draws: **Draw a layer** adds an empty PNG of the
  image's size on top and opens the pixel editor among the other layers, and **Retouch this
  layer** edits a PNG layer in place. The image stays a recipe.
