- **Added**: Nexo items show their own texture in the editor (library, item picker, captured items, slots) instead of one shared placeholder. The server reads each item's texture, or its model's, from `plugins/Nexo/` (external packs included, no Nexo API) and the editor gets all of them in one request, not sent again while nothing changed. A 3D model shows one of its faces, an animated texture its first frame. New: `GET /api/v1/user/projects/:projectId/nexo-item-icons`, WS `nexo_item_icons_fetch`.
