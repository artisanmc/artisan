- **Fixed**: A captured item lost its name and its lore in the editor on Paper 1.21.5 and later (the preview showed the material's name and no lore), most visibly on Nexo items. The server writes text components in Minecraft's own notation (SNBT) since that version; the editor now reads it, and recognizes a Nexo, Oraxen or ItemsAdder id stored under `PublicBukkitValues`. A captured item named only by its item name (Nexo's `itemname`) also gets that name as its label.
