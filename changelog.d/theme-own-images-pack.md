- **Added**: a theme's own images are in the pack. An image under `assets/` of a theme's folder
  (`plugins/Artisan/themes/<theme>/assets/`) is named `<theme>:<name>` in a menu, a title or a
  module's file, while the server holds the theme, in place or not. It enters the pack only when
  named, and shows as drawn: the themes in place do not repaint it. The editor does not show one
  yet.
