- **Added**: Nexo items with a 3D model (furniture, custom blocks, Blockbench models) show a 3D icon in the editor, drawn like the game's inventory does (the model's `display.gui`), instead of one face of their texture. The server sends each model and each texture once; the editor draws them in the background, over the flat icons shown first, and keeps the flat icon of a model it cannot draw. New: `GET /api/v1/user/projects/:projectId/nexo-item-models`, WS `nexo_item_models_fetch`.
