- **Changed**: Selecting on a menu works like Figma. A click picks the topmost thing under the
  pointer: a title text or image when its frame is hit, otherwise the slot's item; a selected text
  or image keeps the hand and is dragged from inside its frame even over a button. **Ctrl+click**
  (**⌘-click**) now selects the next thing under the pointer (an item under a text stays
  reachable); **Shift+click** remains the way to select several items. An image covering the whole
  window is only picked by Ctrl+click or from the layer list, and hidden layers never are.
