- **Fixed**: An underlined or struck title text (or label) placed away from the vanilla title line,
  or at another size, had its line drawn on the vanilla title line ("Jour 5" underlined at y 20,
  size 12, underlined "Récompenses quotidiennes" instead), and the editor drew no line at all. The
  plugin now draws these lines itself with bar glyphs of the text's font, under or through the
  text, in its colour; the editor preview draws them too. The resource pack carries the bars
  (about 24 KB) once some text uses these styles.
