---
title: Sign in
description: Sign in with a password, Google, or Discord.
---

Choose **Continue with Google** on the sign-in or sign-up page, then select your Google account. The button appears when Google sign-in has been configured by the site administrator.

If your verified Gmail address matches an existing Artisan account, Google is linked to that account automatically. Your projects, access, preferences and password stay available. Verified Google Workspace addresses work the same way. Otherwise, a new account is created.

Once linked, your Google identity continues to open the same Artisan account even if your Google email changes. A different Google account cannot replace an existing link automatically. Email matching ignores case and surrounding spaces; it does not merge Gmail aliases containing dots or `+` suffixes.

For an external address used with Google (for example, a non-Workspace company address), automatic linking to an existing Artisan account is unavailable. Use your Artisan password, or **Forgot password?** to recover access.

If you cancel Google sign-in or the attempt expires, return to the sign-in page and try again. Discord, when enabled, also shares your existing Artisan account when its verified email matches.

## Delete your account

In your profile, choose **Delete my account**, then **Send confirmation email**.
Open the emailed link within 30 minutes and choose **Permanently delete my account**.
Opening the link alone does not delete anything. Each link works once; requesting another invalidates the previous one. Requests are limited to one per minute.

Deletion removes your account, private drafts and assistant history, and revokes your sessions.
Networks where you are the last owner are removed from Artisan, including their members' access and server links. Networks with another owner remain available.
**Files on your Minecraft servers stay intact.** You can link those servers again from another account.
If a network that would be removed has an active subscription, cancel it in billing first.

If the email fails to send, the profile shows an error and your account stays active. For an expired link, return to your profile and request another email.
