---
title: Free and Pro editor access
description: How the personal resource quota and Pro access work.
---

Your content stays available in game, even without a subscription. Menus, dialogs and commands already installed on your server continue to work.

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Personal resources per server | 10 | 250 |
| Servers per Network | 2 | 10 |
| Collaborators per Network (besides one owner) | 2 | Unlimited |
| AI credits included | Purchased separately | 500 each month |

Resources supplied by registered modules are outside the personal resource limit. Your Project displays the current personal resource count.

At the limit, you can continue editing existing resources but cannot create another. Above your current plan's limit, personal resources become read-only until the count drops; Free networks can also move to Pro for a higher limit. Your server keeps running and the files remain on disk. Drafts can be kept locally, but publishing checks the quota again.

Pro raises the limits for its Network during the paid period. Canceling stops renewal but keeps Pro access until the paid period ends. Existing servers and collaborators are retained when Pro ends; new additions follow Free limits. The current limits and available Pro price appear on the [pricing page](/pricing).

Artisan Pro costs €9 per month or €70 per year, per Network. Annual billing saves €38 compared with twelve monthly payments. Checkout becomes available when the corresponding subscription is activated.

Both billing options grant 500 AI credits each month. Annual subscribers receive them monthly throughout the paid year, rather than all at once.
