---
title: Free and Pro editor access
description: How the personal resource quota and Pro access work.
---

Click your user card at the bottom of the sidebar to view **Resource usage** for
the selected server. This section shows the personal resource count, plan limit,
and any quota alerts across all its projects, even without an open project. Switching
servers switches the counter; an unavailable inventory is never shown as zero. The current Network's
plan name appears beneath your name on the user card.

Your content stays available in game, even without a subscription. Menus, dialogs and commands already installed on your server continue to work.

Every Network uses **Free** by default. Limits apply automatically from its current
plan; no administrator needs to enable editor restrictions separately.

An administrator can also assign a complete plan without payment, indefinitely
or until a chosen date. Its included credits renew normally. The Credits page
shows the assignment and its expiration; without another entitlement, the
Network returns to Free when the assignment expires or is removed.

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Personal resources per server | 10 | 250 |
| Servers per Network | 2 | 10 |
| Collaborators per Network (besides one owner) | 2 | Unlimited |
| AI credits included | Purchased separately | 500 each month |

Resources supplied by registered modules are outside the personal resource limit. Your Project displays the current personal resource count.

At the limit, you can continue editing existing resources but cannot create another. Above your current plan's limit, personal resources become read-only until the count drops; Free networks can also move to Pro for a higher limit. Your server keeps running and the files remain on disk. Drafts can be kept locally, but publishing checks the quota again.

Pro raises the limits for its Network during the paid period. Canceling stops renewal but keeps Pro access until the paid period ends. Existing servers and collaborators are retained when Pro ends; new additions follow Free limits. The current limits and available Pro price appear on the [pricing page](/pricing).

Artisan Pro costs €9 per month or €70 per year, per Network. Annual billing saves €38 compared with twelve monthly payments. The standard offers are installed automatically and remain visible when payments are not configured. Checkout becomes available once payment setup is ready.

Both billing options grant 500 AI credits each month. Annual subscribers receive them monthly throughout the paid year, rather than all at once.

A custom offer can provide different limits, credits and pricing for your Network. It appears in that Network's billing page, not on public Pricing. Your confirmed subscription keeps its own conditions when a newer offer is published. Creating or publishing an offer alone does not activate paid access.
